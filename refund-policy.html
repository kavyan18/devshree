Return & Refund Policy

Clear, transparent policies for replacements, damaged supplies in transit, and refund reimbursements.

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee

At Devshree, we stand behind the durability and factory quality of every item we supply. You are entitled to request a replacement or return within 7 days from the date of physical delivery under the following conditions:

  • The item was received in a physically damaged or broken state.
  • The item has an intrinsic manufacturing defect or functional malfunction.
  • The item delivered differs in model, brand, specification, or finish from what was invoiced.
  • Missing components, screws, accessories, or parts mentioned in the manufacturer package.

2. Eligible vs. Non-Returnable Items

Due to the nature of construction and home improvement supplies, certain conditions apply:

  • Eligible for Return: Unused brass hardware, unopened factory-sealed switch boxes, valves, and tools in original packaging with intact warranty barcodes.
  • Non-Returnable:
    • Custom tinted or computerized machine-mixed wall paint emulsions.
    • Electrical wires or plumbing conduits that have been cut, installed, or spliced on-site.
    • Items showing clear signs of physical abuse, incorrect electrical load installation, or unauthorized tampering.

3. Order Cancellation Policy

You can cancel any order free of charge at any time prior to warehouse packaging and carrier handover.

To cancel an order, simply click "My Account" on the website, or send a quick WhatsApp message to our Trade Desk at +91 88661 30409 quoting your Invoice Number (`INV-DEV-...`). If payment was made via UPI QR, the complete amount will be refunded immediately.

4. Refund Method & Timelines

Once a returned item is received at our Rajkot hub and inspected:

  • UPI QR Payments: The full purchase amount is refunded directly back to your source UPI VPA or bank account within 2 to 4 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: Our customer support desk will request your preferred bank account details (Account Number + IFSC) or UPI VPA to process an instant electronic IMPS / NEFT transfer within 24 to 48 hours of return approval.
  • GST Credit Note: For registered contractor GSTIN accounts, an official Indian GST Credit Note will be generated and issued alongside the refund for tax reconciliation.
