Shipping & Delivery Policy

Everything you need to know about dispatch schedules, delivery timelines, tracking, and shipping fees for home and contractor orders.

1. Warehouse Dispatch Schedule

All hardware, electricals, plumbing supplies, and paints are packed and dispatched directly from our central fulfillment hub located at B-13, Jainam Apartment, Jamnagar Road, Rajkot, Gujarat - 360006.

Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST (Monday through Saturday) are typically processed and handed over to courier carriers on the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on Sundays/Public Holidays will be dispatched on the next business working day.

2. Estimated Delivery Timelines by Region

Region / Destination Major Cities Estimated Transit Time
Gujarat (Local & State) Rajkot, Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Jamnagar, Bhavnagar 1 – 2 Business Days
Western & Central India Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Indore, Bhopal, Jaipur 2 – 3 Business Days
North, South & East Metros Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Lucknow 3 – 5 Business Days
Rest of India & Project Sites Tier-3 Towns, Rural construction sites, Northeast India 5 – 7 Business Days

3. Shipping Rates & Free Delivery Threshold

  • Orders Above ₹1,999: 100% FREE express delivery across India.
  • Orders Under ₹1,999: A nominal flat rate of ₹99 is added to cover protective packaging and secure logistics transit.
  • Bulky / Trade Heavy Materials: For heavy industrial bulk orders (e.g. 50+ boxes of pipes or 20L paint buckets), customized freight transport is coordinated directly with the contractor via our Rajkot dispatch desk.

4. Live Consignment Tracking

As soon as your package is scanned by our carrier, you will receive an automated WhatsApp notification and SMS with your Delhivery AWB number. You can also track your consignment in real-time on our website at any time using your Invoice Number (`INV-DEV-...`).

5. Transit Damage Protection

We take extreme care in packaging hardware, delicate porcelain fixtures, and brass valves with multi-layer bubble wrap and heavy corrugated boxes. In the unlikely event that your package arrives with physical damage or seal tampering, please photograph the parcel and notify us within 48 hours at +91 88661 30409 or support@devshree.in for an immediate replacement.
