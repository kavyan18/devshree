Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026 • Effective for all Devshree Hardware & Home Supplies customers, builders, and contractors.

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to DEVSHREE Supplies Private Limited ("Devshree", "we", "our", or "us"). We operate the e-commerce platform located at https://devshree.in and affiliated subdomains, supplying high-performance electricals, architectural hardware, plumbing fixtures, and wall paints across India.

This Privacy Policy sets out how Devshree collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

2. Information We Collect

To process hardware orders, generate legal Indian GST invoices, and coordinate express consignments, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Identity Data: Full name, email address, password hashes (secured via Firebase Authentication), and Indian mobile number (+91).
  • Delivery & Logistics Data: 6-digit Indian Postal PIN code, street address, city, state, building/site details, and landmark.
  • Commercial & B2B GST Data: For registered trade contractors and builders, we collect Business Legal Name, Registered Trade Name, and 15-character Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) to enable Input Tax Credit (ITC) under Indian tax laws.
  • Transaction & Order History: Records of purchased supplies, quantities, transaction IDs, invoice numbers (`INV-DEV-YYYY-XXXX`), and preferred payment method (COD or UPI QR). We do not store full credit/debit card numbers or bank net-banking passwords on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

Devshree uses your personal information strictly for legitimate commercial and fulfillment purposes:

  • Processing, packing, and dispatching hardware materials to your specified destination.
  • Generating official 18% GST Tax Invoices compliant with Central and State GST regulations.
  • Sending real-time order alerts, dispatch AWB codes, and delivery status updates via automated WhatsApp messages and SMS.
  • Facilitating store owner dispatch alerts to our Rajkot distribution hub.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraudulent transactions or security violations.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to marketing third parties. We share information solely with verified logistical and cloud infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality:

  • Logistics Partners: Delhivery Express, Blue Dart, and Indian Speed Post for order transit.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform & Firebase (Firestore Database, Authentication) for secure encrypted data persistence.
  • Government & Tax Authorities: When required under statutory laws (e.g., GST e-invoicing compliance or court orders).

5. Data Security Measures

Devshree employs industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption, tokenized authentication, secure role-based Cloud Firestore rules, and access control mechanisms to safeguard your records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
